    Experience

    2026 to now

    AI and automation engineer

    Motive Journey, Cork, Ireland (remote)

    I architect the platform’s backend and AI services as one of five engineers across AI, software, and frontend. It is a set of modular FastAPI microservices with Celery, Redis, PostgreSQL, and Docker, versioned in Git, shipped through CircleCI pipelines, orchestrated with Kubernetes, and deployed across Vercel, Hostinger, and Google Cloud. Redis does four jobs: Celery broker, pub/sub event bus between services, cache for low-latency reads, and store for idempotency and token-revocation checks.

    The automated agents run on Google Gemini through Vertex AI, using tool and function calling orchestrated with LangGraph for multi-step workflows. I built human-like voice chatbots with real-time speech in and out through ElevenLabs, plus corrective, procedural, hybrid, and agentic RAG pipelines that combine SPLADE sparse retrieval, HNSW and FAISS dense search, and ColBERT late-interaction reranking. I also build the CRM and business apps on the platform.

    Grafana dashboards watch system health, and releases go through unit, end-to-end, smoke, submission, and real-world simulation tests, stress tests in JMeter, and API tests in Postman. The largest system I’ve built there is MJ-OS.

    2024 to 2026

    AI software engineer

    ARAS (Applied Robotics and AI Solutions), K. N. Toosi University of Technology

    I joined ARAS to build production-grade AI applications on Django and FastAPI. I started by turning research outputs into dependable backends, including models such as the ROP pipeline that other lab members had developed, and hardened them with clean APIs, observability, and CI/CD.

    I then joined the lab’s AI research group under Professor Hamid D. Taghirad, training machine learning and deep learning models on high-stakes, real-world medical datasets.

    From there I moved into agentic AI, studying tool-using agents, retrieval, and evaluation loops to make systems reliable beyond demos. That shift produced my two flagship projects: the occupational health platform, and SurgiNote, our hackathon training assistant. Both were delivered as deployable services.

    Throughout, I worked directly with medical specialists who reviewed and corrected the systems’ outputs, so the flagship projects met the standards of clinical practice. The work included a dedicated three-month internship.
